Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Mupitsu Clips is a private, single-user publishing tool. This policy explains what it does with data.

1. Who we are

Mupitsu Clips is operated by its owner, who is also the only user of the Service. Contact: contact@mupitsu.clips.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok authorisation data: the OAuth access token, refresh token and the open ID of the TikTok account that authorised the Service. These are used only to call TikTok's API on behalf of that account.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok's user.info.basic scope (open ID, display name, avatar), used to display which account is connected.
  • Content: the video files and captions that the owner chooses to publish, together with their publishing status.

3. Data we do not collect

The Service does not collect data about TikTok viewers or other users. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no public user accounts.

4. Where data is stored

Tokens and publishing records are stored locally on the operator's computer, in files excluded from version control. Video files are stored locally until they are uploaded to TikTok.

5. Sharing

Data is shared with TikTok only, as part of the publishing requests made through its official API. No data is sold or shared with any other party.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept until the authorisation is revoked. Publishing records are kept for as long as they are useful for statistics. Deleting the local data folder removes everything.

7. Your rights

The account holder can revoke the Service's access at any time from the TikTok app or website ("Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions"). Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens immediately.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.
