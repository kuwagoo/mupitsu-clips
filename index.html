Mupitsu Clips

Twitch highlights, cut into vertical clips.

Mupitsu Clips is a personal publishing tool. It takes clip highlights from Twitch channels we own or that have authorised us, formats them as vertical videos, and publishes them to our own TikTok account @mupitsu through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

What the tool does

  • authorises once with TikTok (Login Kit) for the account we own;
  • uploads a finished vertical video (MP4, H.264) as a direct post;
  • sends the caption and privacy level chosen by the account owner;
  • keeps the account owner in control: nothing is posted without that explicit authorisation.

Scope

The tool only ever acts on the single TikTok account that authorised it. It does not read other users' data, does not post on anyone else's behalf and has no public sign-up.
